# Decision Record

- Decision ID: DEC-011
- Stage: 07-adr
- Title: Amend freshness target after experiment M1
- Decision: Adopt p95 data age at most 75 s (METRIC-001, REQ-N01) and poll standings just after the source cache expires (NFR-001, ADR-004)
- Context: Codeforces serves standings from a 60 s proxy cache with an Expires header; no polling strategy can show data younger than about 60 to 63 s at p95
- Chosen option: p95 at most 75 s with expiry-aligned polling
- Rationale: A 60 s promise cannot be kept; 75 s leaves margin for processing and Telegram edits and polling once per minute is cheaper and no worse than faster polling
- Impact: Medium
- Reversibility: High
- Risk: MEDIUM
- Evidence: EVID-019,EVID-020,EVID-021
- Approval required: yes
- Approval status: approved
